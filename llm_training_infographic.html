05 — Training Phases Deep Dive
01
Pre-Training
The compute-intensive phase: the model learns language structure, world knowledge, and reasoning patterns by predicting the next token across a massive corpus. This is where 90%+ of compute is spent.
Causal LM BFloat16 AdamW Flash Attention Tensor Parallel
02
Supervised Fine-Tuning
Human-curated instruction-response pairs are used to teach the model to follow instructions and produce helpful, structured outputs. Typically 100K–1M high-quality examples. Much less compute than pre-training.
Instruction Tuning Chat Templates Low LR Human Labels
03
RLHF / Constitutional AI
Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (OpenAI/Google) or Constitutional AI (Anthropic) fine-tune values, helpfulness, and safety. A reward model is trained on human preference data, then PPO or GRPO optimizes policy.
PPO / GRPO Reward Model CAI Preference Data
04
Evaluation & Red-Teaming
Models are tested on MMLU, HumanEval, GSM8K, BIG-Bench, and proprietary benchmarks. Red teams attempt adversarial jailbreaks. Safety evaluations for dangerous capabilities are mandatory at frontier labs.
MMLU HumanEval GSM8K Red Team HELM
05
Inference Optimization
Quantization (INT8/INT4), speculative decoding, and KV-cache optimizations reduce serving costs by 2–10×. Distillation creates smaller student models. Crucial for economic viability at scale (millions of daily users).
Quantization Distillation vLLM Spec Decoding
06
Deployment & Monitoring
Models deploy via APIs with load balancing, rate limiting, and usage monitoring. Continuous feedback loops identify failures for future fine-tuning cycles. Model cards and system cards document capabilities and risks.
API Gateway A/B Testing Model Card Feedback Loop
