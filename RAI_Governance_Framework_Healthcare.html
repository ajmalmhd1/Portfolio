Section 03 — AI Vetting Processes

Clinical AI Vetting Processes

Medicare Health System applies a structured clinical review process before any AI model is used in patient care — whether built internally or procured from a vendor. Both tracks require Ethics Board approval and clinical validation before any patient-facing deployment.

Internally Developed Clinical AI

1
Clinical Use Case Registration Model team registers: intended clinical use, target patient population, EHR data sources, clinical sponsor (physician champion), and anticipated patient impact. Filed in the AI Governance Registry.
2
Risk Tier & FDA SaMD Classification CAEO and Risk Manager classify under NIST AI RMF tiers and FDA SaMD risk categories (Class I/II/III). High-risk diagnostic or treatment models trigger full FDA pre-market pathway review and Predetermined Change Control Plan drafting.
3
HIPAA & PHI Privacy Impact Assessment Privacy Specialist assesses patient consent, HIPAA de-identification method (Safe Harbor or Expert Determination), data minimization, and HITECH breach notification applicability. PIA filed before any PHI enters training pipelines.
4
Health Equity & Bias Audit Fairness Engineer tests model for disparate outcomes across race, ethnicity, sex, age, disability, and insurance status using CMS disparity metrics. Adverse Impact Ratio must be ≥ 0.80 for all protected groups.
5
Clinical Validation Study Physician champion and Clinical Informatics run retrospective and/or prospective validation on Medicare Health System's own patient population. External validation required for all Tier 1 models prior to Ethics Board submission.
6
Clinical AI Ethics Board Approval Gate Tier 1 models (diagnostic support, triage, treatment recommendation) require full board vote including physician and patient advocate. Tier 2 requires CAEO sign-off. No model touches patient data or care workflows without written approval.
7
Staged Clinical Deployment & Monitoring MLOps deploys: shadow mode → single clinical unit pilot → system-wide rollout. Clinical performance dashboards, health equity alerts, and clinician feedback channels active from day one in production.

Externally Procured Clinical AI

1
Vendor AI Disclosure & Model Card Request Procurement issues a standardized AI Disclosure Questionnaire: training data demographics, patient population represented, FDA clearance status (510(k) / De Novo), known failure modes, and independent validation evidence.
2
HIPAA BAA & Regulatory Due Diligence Legal executes HIPAA Business Associate Agreement. Risk Manager verifies FDA 510(k) or De Novo clearance, SOC 2 Type II, ONC Health IT certification, and 21st Century Cures Act information-blocking compliance.
3
Independent Health Equity Validation Medicare Health System's Fairness Engineer tests the vendor model on our own patient population for disparate outcomes. We do not rely solely on vendor-supplied performance metrics or training demographics.
4
Local Clinical Validation Physician champion runs retrospective validation on our patient cohort. Models trained on different health systems or demographics require local re-validation — a known limitation of many commercial clinical AI products.
5
AI-Specific Contractual Obligations Legal embeds clauses: audit rights, clinician override capability, model drift notification SLAs (≤72 hrs), PHI deletion rights on contract termination, adverse event disclosure windows, and patient right-to-explanation.
6
Clinical AI Ethics Board Review Vendor tools used in patient diagnosis, risk stratification, or treatment decisions require the same Ethics Board gate as internal models. Board reviews vendor clinical evidence, equity testing, and failure mode analysis.
7
Annual Re-Assessment & FDA Change Protocol All clinical AI vendors undergo full re-assessment annually, or within 30 days of: a model update, patient safety event, FDA enforcement action, or significant demographic shift in performance metrics.
⚠ Clinical AI Ethics Board Approval Gate — All Tier 1 clinical AI models (diagnostic support, risk stratification, treatment recommendation, prior authorization) require written Ethics Board approval before any patient-facing deployment. The CAEO and CMO hold joint veto authority. No exceptions.

Section 05 — Key Performance Indicators

Clinical RAI Key Performance Indicators

Following Ethical Machines best practices, every KPI is SMART and tied directly to Medicare Health System's stated AI ethical principles. Each missed target triggers a specific named action with an owner and deadline — ensuring accountability at the point of patient care risk.

🏥 Patient Safety & Clinical Quality
KPIDefinitionCadenceTargetAction if Missed
AI-Contributed Adverse Event Rate Reportable patient safety events where an AI recommendation was a contributing factor, per 10,000 AI-touched clinical encounters Monthly ≤ 1 per 10k Immediate clinical suspension; root-cause review within 24 hrs; CMO and Safety Officer notification
Clinician Override Rate % of AI recommendations overridden by clinical staff. Sustained low rate signals automation bias risk; sustained high rate signals poor model fit. Monthly 5% – 30% Outside band: investigate automation bias (low) or model relevance (high); adjust clinical workflow integration
Clinical Validation Currency % of Tier 1 clinical AI models with a validation study on Medicare Health System's patient population completed within the past 24 months Quarterly 100% Move to shadow mode only; suspend patient-facing use until re-validated by physician champion
⚖️ Health Equity & Fairness
KPIDefinitionCadenceTargetAction if Missed
Adverse Impact Ratio — Clinical Positive prediction rate for protected class ÷ rate for reference group (race, ethnicity, sex, insurance status) per clinical risk and diagnostic models, using CMS disparity metrics Monthly, per model ≥ 0.80 Freeze clinical use; bias audit within 5 business days; Ethics Board and CMO notification
Health Equity Audit Coverage % of Tier 1 + Tier 2 clinical models with a completed, documented health equity audit in the past 12 months Quarterly 100% Block patient-facing deployment; model placed in audit queue with 30-day resolution deadline
Patient Disparity Complaint Rate % of patient grievances alleging discriminatory AI-informed care decisions vs. total AI-touched encounters, tracked per CMS quality reporting Quarterly < 0.2% Escalate to CAEO and Patient Advocate Board member; root-cause investigation within 10 business days
🔬 Transparency & Explainability
KPIDefinitionCadenceTargetAction if Missed
Clinician Explanation Availability % of Tier 1 clinical AI outputs accompanied by a SHAP-based clinician-readable rationale accessible within the EHR workflow at point of care Monthly 100% Halt clinical deployment; Explainability Lead SLA: integrate explanations within 14 days
Patient AI Disclosure Rate % of patients whose care involved a Tier 1 AI model who received an ONC-compliant disclosure of AI use in clinical summary or consent documentation per 21st Century Cures Act Monthly 100% Suspend patient-facing use; Legal and Privacy review within 48 hrs for HIPAA compliance risk
Clinician AI Trust Score % of clinical staff who rate AI model explanations as "clinically useful" or better in supporting their decision-making, measured via validated survey Semi-annually ≥ 70% Redesign EHR integration and explanation UX; re-survey within 60 days; escalate findings to CMO
🛡️ Governance Process Integrity
KPIDefinitionCadenceTargetAction if Missed
Ethics Board Review Rate % of Tier 1 clinical AI deployments with documented Ethics Board approval before any patient-facing use Per deployment 100% Immediate rollback; post-incident review; CMO and Board notification within 48 hrs
Vetting Cycle Time Avg. calendar days from clinical AI registration to Ethics Board decision for Tier 2 models Monthly ≤ 35 days Identify bottleneck in vetting pipeline; resource reallocation or process streamlining within 15 days
Vendor BAA Compliance Rate % of external clinical AI vendors with a current HIPAA BAA on file AND completed annual re-assessment on schedule Annually 100% Suspend vendor PHI data flow and clinical use until BAA is executed and assessment completed
🔒 Data Privacy & HIPAA Compliance
KPIDefinitionCadenceTargetAction if Missed
PHI Privacy Impact Assessment Rate % of new or significantly updated clinical AI models with a completed HIPAA-aligned PIA on file before any PHI enters training or inference pipelines Quarterly 100% Block PHI access to model; Privacy Specialist SLA: PIA completed within 10 business days
AI-Related HIPAA Breach Incidents Number of HIPAA breach notification events per year where an AI system was the primary or contributing cause of unauthorized PHI disclosure Annually 0 Immediate incident response protocol; HITECH 60-day breach notification clock starts; OCR notification if threshold met
Clinical RAI Training Completion % of clinical and technical staff who interact with AI who completed annual HIPAA AI training and RAI ethics module Annually ≥ 95% Restrict clinical AI system access after 30-day grace period; report non-compliance to department heads
Ethical Machines KPI Principle Applied: Every KPI triggers a specific named action with an owner and deadline — not a passive "review." Targets are threshold-based and calibrated to clinical risk thresholds so that outcomes are unambiguous, actionable, and traceable to patient safety outcomes.
