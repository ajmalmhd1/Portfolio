
                               Assignment 4.4- Explainable AI (XAI)

This project showcases a visually engaging infographic on Explainable Artificial Intelligence (XAI), designed to simplify complex AI concepts.
It highlights the importance of transparency and interpretability in modern machine learning systems.
The infographic explains how XAI helps users understand how AI models make decisions.
It covers key ideas such as black-box models, feature importance, and model transparency.
The project also emphasizes ethical concerns like bias detection and accountability in AI.
It is built using HTML and CSS with a focus on clean and effective visual storytelling.
The design aims to make technical content accessible to both technical and non-technical audiences.
This work reflects an understanding of both AI concepts and frontend presentation skills.
It demonstrates the ability to communicate complex ideas in a simple and engaging format.

You can view the live infographic here: https://ajmalmhd1.github.io/Portfolio/xai_infographic.html
