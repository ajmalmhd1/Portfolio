Section 03 — AI Vetting Processes

AI Product Vetting Processes

NovaPay applies a structured review process before any AI model is deployed, whether built internally or procured from a third-party vendor. Both tracks converge on an Ethics Board approval gate before production release.

Internally Developed AI

1
Use Case Registration Model team registers the use case in the AI Governance Registry. Defines intended use, data sources, affected populations, and business owner.
2
Risk Tier Classification CAEO and Risk Manager classify the model (Tier 1 High / Tier 2 Medium / Tier 3 Low) based on regulatory impact, consumer harm potential, and scale.
3
Bias & Fairness Audit AI Fairness Engineer runs disparity impact tests across protected classes (race, gender, age). Adverse Impact Ratio must meet or exceed 0.80.
4
Privacy Impact Assessment Data Privacy Specialist evaluates training data for consent, minimization, and GLBA compliance. PIA document filed in governance repository.
5
Explainability Review Model Explainability Lead validates SHAP output and draft adverse-action notices. Customer-readable explanations required for all Tier 1 models.
6
Ethics Board Approval Gate Tier 1 models require full board vote. Tier 2 requires chair sign-off. Tier 3 follows expedited review. No Tier 1 or 2 model deploys without written approval.
7
Staged Deployment & Monitoring MLOps team deploys to shadow mode → 5% traffic → full release. Drift alerts and fairness dashboards active from day one in production.

Externally Procured AI

1
Vendor AI Disclosure Request Procurement issues a standardized AI Disclosure Questionnaire covering model type, training data provenance, known limitations, and regulatory compliance history.
2
Third-Party Risk Assessment AI Risk Manager evaluates vendor responses. Checks for SOC 2 Type II, FCRA compliance (for credit use), and available model documentation or "model cards."
3
Independent Fairness Validation NovaPay's Fairness Engineer tests the vendor model on NovaPay's own customer dataset for disparate impact — we do not rely solely on vendor-supplied metrics.
4
Contractual AI Obligations Legal embeds AI-specific clauses: audit rights, drift notification SLAs, data deletion rights, incident disclosure windows, and right-to-explanation obligations.
5
Ethics Board Review External AI products undergo the same Ethics Board gate as internal models. High-risk vendor tools (e.g., fraud scoring, credit decisioning) require full board approval.
6
Integration Monitoring Setup MLOps team instruments vendor API calls for fairness monitoring. Automated alerts fire if demographic parity drops below threshold or model accuracy degrades.
7
Annual Vendor Re-Assessment All external AI vendors undergo a full re-assessment annually, or within 30 days of a reported model update, data breach, or adverse regulatory finding.
⚠ Ethics Board Approval Gate — All Tier 1 AI products (internal or external) require written Ethics Board approval before any production deployment. The CAEO holds veto authority.

Section 05 — Key Performance Indicators

RAI Key Performance Indicators

Following Ethical Machines best practices, every KPI is SMART — specific, measurable, actionable, relevant, and time-bound — and tied directly to NovaPay's stated AI ethical principles. Targets trigger defined review actions, not just reports.

🟢 Fairness & Non-Discrimination
KPI Definition Measurement Target Action if Missed
Adverse Impact Ratio (AIR) Approval rate for protected class ÷ approval rate for reference group, per ECOA-relevant models Monthly, per model ≥ 0.80 Freeze model, trigger bias audit within 5 business days
Disparate Complaint Rate % of regulatory complaints alleging discrimination vs. total AI-touched decisions Quarterly < 0.5% Escalate to CAEO; initiate root-cause investigation
Fairness Audit Coverage % of Tier 1 + Tier 2 models with a completed, documented fairness audit in the past 12 months Quarterly 100% Block any non-audited model from production deployment
🔵 Transparency & Explainability
KPI Definition Measurement Target Action if Missed
Adverse Action Notice Accuracy % of AI-driven adverse decisions (credit denial, limit decrease) that include a customer-readable, CFPB-compliant explanation Monthly 100% Halt deployment; legal and explainability review within 48 hrs
Explainability Coverage % of customer-facing Tier 1 models with SHAP-based explanations reviewed and approved by Ethics Board Quarterly 100% Remove model from customer-facing use until coverage achieved
Customer Explanation Satisfaction % of customers surveyed who rated their AI decision explanation as "understandable" or better Semi-annually ≥ 75% Redesign explanation UX; re-survey within 60 days
🟡 Governance Process Integrity
KPI Definition Measurement Target Action if Missed
Ethics Board Review Completion Rate % of Tier 1 model deployments that completed the Ethics Board review process before go-live Per deployment 100% Rollback deployment; initiate post-incident review
Vetting Cycle Time (Internal) Avg. calendar days from model registration to Ethics Board decision for Tier 2 models Monthly ≤ 30 days Identify bottleneck; add resource or streamline process
Vendor Re-Assessment Compliance % of external AI vendors with a completed annual re-assessment on schedule Annually 100% Suspend vendor integration until re-assessment completed
🛡️ Model Safety & Reliability
KPI Definition Measurement Target Action if Missed
Model Drift Alert Response Time Avg. time from automated drift alert to documented investigation start by MLOps team Per alert ≤ 4 hours Escalate to CAEO; auto-throttle model to reduced traffic
PIA Completion Rate % of new or significantly updated AI models with a completed Privacy Impact Assessment on file Quarterly 100% Block production deployment until PIA filed
RAI Training Completion % of employees in AI-building or AI-deploying roles who have completed annual RAI training Annually ≥ 95% Restrict system access for non-compliant employees after 30-day grace
Ethical Machines KPI Principle Applied: Every KPI above triggers a specific, named action — not a "review" — ensuring accountability rather than passive monitoring. Targets are threshold-based, not directional, so outcomes are unambiguous.
